# Interactive observation-policy calibration v1

This bounded synthetic experiment models two hidden machine states with identical ordinary observations but different energy values. Compare passive observation, privileged telemetry, and an active calibration action.

A policy produces a transcript of actions and observations. Energy is identifiable under a policy only when hidden states with different energy cannot produce the same transcript. Permission, observation cost, and measurement perturbation are explicit fields. Hidden energies are synthetic labels; this is not an M3 Max energy measurement.
