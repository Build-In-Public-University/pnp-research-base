# Branching adaptive observation-policy calibration v1

Five synthetic hidden states are identified exactly by a genuinely branching policy. The first cheap local channel resolves two states immediately. Its `uncertain` branch invokes a second local channel; two residual states resolve there, while one final residual branch invokes privileged exact telemetry.

The experiment reports transcript, worst-case cost, expected cost under a declared uniform prior, perturbation, and exact contract satisfaction. Values are synthetic modeled units, not hardware measurements.
