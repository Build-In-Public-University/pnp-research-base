# Loss-aware adaptive observation calibration v1

A finite-loss decision contract chooses `safe` or `escalate`. Hidden states A-D are safe and E is escalate-worthy. The policy observes a cheap branch and then a local refinement; a residual D/E class can be resolved by privileged telemetry or acted on under residual uncertainty.

The dynamic program compares stopping risk with observation cost. Two priors/loss settings are tested: a common/high-consequence residual where observation is justified, and a rare/low-consequence residual where rational stopping occurs. Values are synthetic modeled units.
