# Action-relative value-of-information calibration v1

Compare beliefs p=0.1 and p=0.9 in the fragile/sturdy dual-control fixture. Their binary Shannon entropies are equal, but the action-conditioned costs are asymmetric. Compute the immediate stop risk, perfect-information residual risk, information value, and lower-envelope policy costs.

The result tests whether entropy alone selects an attention policy. All costs are synthetic modeled units.
