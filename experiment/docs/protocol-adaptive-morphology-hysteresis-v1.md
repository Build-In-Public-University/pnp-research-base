# Adaptive morphology hysteresis calibration v1

A capability can be remote or locally embodied. For demand d per period, remote service costs 3d and local service plus maintenance costs d+2. Switching costs are 10 from remote to local and 6 from local to remote. Evaluate finite horizons and demand levels with exact dynamic programming. The experiment tests whether switching costs produce distinct enter and exit thresholds rather than one instantaneous crossover.
