# Rights and attribution review

This is a local analysis package, not a declaration that every included source may be redistributed.

- The supplied IPM, Boussinesq, Euler and statement PDFs retain their authorship and original contents. Redistribution status is unverified.
- The blog and Clay documents retain the source URLs in `sources/web/`. Extraction does not confer a new license.
- User-supplied P/NP notes and the conversation are included at the user's request for local preparation. Public release remains a separate approval step.
- Experiment code and generated receipts preserve the source project snapshot and its existing notices. No new blanket license is applied to third-party sources.
- Operational skill bodies are omitted rather than redistributing unrelated private workflow material.

Before publication, confirm rights or replace restricted full-text copies with citations. Record substitutions in the manifest and boundary; do not call a citation-only package a complete full-text archive.
